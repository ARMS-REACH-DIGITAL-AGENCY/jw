No. This is not therapy, medical treatment, crisis support, or a replacement for professional mental healthcare.

It is marketed as coaching so people understand the category. Jason's specific coaching method is Direct Pointing.

What happens on the free call?

The call determines fit. You can explain what you are dealing with, ask questions, and decide whether private or group sessions make sense.

Who is this not for?

This is not for someone in crisis, someone seeking diagnosis, or someone who needs licensed clinical care.
