Why most people stay stuck

More information does not always create more freedom.

Most people do not stay stuck because they are lazy, weak, or lacking answers.

They stay stuck because the same hidden assumptions keep shaping how they see themselves, their thoughts, their relationships, and their future.

Books, podcasts, therapy, meditation, and coaching can all help. But if they are aimed at the wrong target, the same loop eventually returns.

Direct Pointing is designed to question the assumptions underneath the struggle, not just manage the symptoms on top of it.
